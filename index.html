    Engineering Foundation

    The 11-Stage Perception Pipeline

    From raw camera sensor arrays to synthesized spoken advice, each frame flows through an optimized high-throughput edge pipeline.

    Stage 01

    Sensor Ingestion

    High-framerate hardware video capture utilizing zero-copy OffscreenCanvas buffers.

    Stage 02

    YOLO11n Detection

    Sub-15ms bounding box and class prediction accelerated with ONNX WebGPU shaders.

    Stage 03

    Geometry Filtering

    Class acceptance floors, aspect ratio limits, and edge noise boundary rejection.

    Stage 04

    ByteTrack Association

    Kalman filter track persistence preserving stable obstacle identities across turns.

    Stage 05

    Dense Depth Anything

    Monocular foundation model estimating dense 3D structural geometry and walking surfaces.

    Stage 06

    3D Spatial Fusion

    Physics contrast verification (Droi - Dmargin) eliminating flat-wall phantoms.

    Stage 07

    Sector Allocation

    Geometric partitioning into Left (0.0-0.35), Center (0.35-0.65), and Right (0.65-1.0).

    Stage 08

    Pathway Analyzer

    Dense free-space corridor analysis detecting non-COCO hazards (doors, stairs, drops).

    Stage 09

    Hazard Priority

    Kinematic hazard scoring factoring time-to-impact, approach velocity, and sector weight.

    Stage 10

    Temporal Gate

    Confirmation hysteresis filter guaranteeing zero spurious alerts before speaking.

    Stage 11

    Audio & Haptics

    Prioritized text-to-speech with rate modulation and directional haptic vibration pulses.

    The COCO Blindness Breakthrough

    Standard object detectors only identify 80 pre-trained COCO classes. VisionNav AI evaluates dense monocular depth maps to identify physical obstacles that YOLO cannot see.
